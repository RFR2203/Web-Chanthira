What is
Solid Perfume?

Solid perfume is a portable, wax-based fragrance that melts gently with your skin's warmth. Made with natural ingredients and minimal alcohol, it offers a subtle, long-lasting scent perfect for on-the-go application. Compact and travel-friendly, it’s your dream fragrance in a pocket-sized form.

About

At Chanthira, we believe that fragrance is more than just a scent—it’s an expression of your personality, your dreams, and your journey. Our brand is dedicated to creating solid perfumes that are as unique and special as you are.

Crafted with care, our perfumes are made from natural ingredients like shea butter, almond oil, and beeswax, ensuring a gentle yet long-lasting fragrance experience. With minimal alcohol content, our products are kind to your skin while delivering a captivating aroma that lasts for hours.

Portable, eco-friendly, and travel-ready, Chanthira solid perfumes are perfect for the modern individual who values simplicity, sustainability, and elegance. Whether you’re looking for a signature scent or a versatile fragrance for every occasion, Chanthira is here to help you capture the scent of your dreams.

Welcome to Chanthira—where nature meets craftsmanship, and every fragrance tells a story.
